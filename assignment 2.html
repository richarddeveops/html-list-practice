Ingredients for pounded yam and egusi

  • Yam
  • Ground egusi
  • Palm oil
  • Vegetables
  • Meat
  • Onion and pepper
  • Salt and seasoning
  • Water

Preparation steps

Step 1
Peel, wash, and cut the yam into pieces.
Step 2
Boil the yam until soft, then pound it until smooth.
Step 3
Cook the meat with onion, seasoning, and a little water.
Step 4
Prepare the egusi with palm oil, pepper, and some of the meat stock.
Step 5
Add the meat and vegetables, cook until tender, and serve with the pounded yam.
