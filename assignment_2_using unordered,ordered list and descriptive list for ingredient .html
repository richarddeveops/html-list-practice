Steps in preparing Pounded Yam🥔, egusi, vegetables☘ and meat 🍖🍗🥩.

  • Step 1
    Peel the yam, wash it, and cut it into medium-sized chunks.
  • Step 2
    Boil the yam in water until it is soft enough for a knife to pass through easily.
  • Step 3
    Drain the water and pound the yam in a mortar&pestle until it becomes smooth and stretchy, with no lumps.
  • Step 4
    Season and boil the meat with onion, seasoning cubes, and salt until tender; keep the stock.
  • Step 5
    Heat palm oil in a pot, then add chopped onion, and pepper, and fry briefly.
  • Step 6
    Stir in the ground egusi and fry for a few minutes, adding a little stock of water to form a thick paste.
  • Step 7
    Add the cooked meat, and remaining stock, then simmer until the egusi is well cooked.
  • Step 8
    Add the washed vegetables, season to taste, and simmer for a few more minutes.
  • Step 9
    Dish out the pounded yam and serve hot with the egusi soup.
  • Step 10
    Enjoy your meal!
